Welcome to Paris

Paris is one of the most beautiful and famous cities in the world. It is known for its architecture, art and culture.

History

Paris has a long and fascinating history. It has played an important role in European art, culture and politics.

Famous Places

The Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum and Notre-Dame are among the famous attractions of Paris.

French Food

Paris is famous for delicious pastries, bread, cheese and traditional French cuisine.
